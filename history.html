History

Pierce the Veil started as brothers making music in San Diego, California.

The band

Vic Fuentes (vocals, guitar) and his younger brother Mike Fuentes (drums) formed the band in 2006. Tony Perry (guitar) and Jaime Preciado (bass) round out the lineup. Vic's high, urgent voice and Mike's fast, busy drumming became the sound people recognize in seconds.

Timeline

  1. 2006Vic and Mike form Pierce the Veil in San Diego.
  2. 2007Debut album A Flair for the Dramatic arrives.
  3. 2010Selfish Machines brings a bigger sound and a wider audience, including the fan favorite "Caraphernelia."
  4. 2012Collide with the Sky becomes the breakthrough, led by "King for a Day" and "Bulletproof Love."
  5. 2016Misadventures adds "The Divine Zero" and "Texas Is Forever."
  6. 2023The Jaws of Life marks their return after a long wait, with "Pass the Nirvana."

Why it matters

The band helped define a generation of alternative and post-hardcore music. Their shows are loud sing-alongs where thousands of people shout every word, which is a big part of why fans stay loyal for years.
