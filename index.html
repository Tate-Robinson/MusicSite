Pierce
the Veil

Four musicians from San Diego who turned heartbreak, nerves, and big choruses into some of the loudest, most honest rock of the last twenty years.

Pierce the Veil mixes post-hardcore energy with soaring melodies and lyrics that are all feeling. This site is a small tribute to the band and the songs that got me hooked.
